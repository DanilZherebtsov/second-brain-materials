# Job opening: ML/LLM engineer, Evo-ai (project Hermes)

*Internal recruiting document. Version 1.0. June 2026.*
*Opened for our flagship project — an AI support assistant for Hermes Logistics Ltd (brand "Hermes"). Go-live in production — by 01.10.2026.*

---

## What we are looking for

An ML/LLM engineer for the project Hermes team — building an AI support assistant (LLM+RAG over the Hermes knowledge base: policies, tariffs, shipment statuses, FAQ; integration with their helpdesk). This is the core of the assistant: RAG, prompting, quality evaluation. We work remotely, core team on CET, two-week sprint rhythm. Evo-ai builds AI that works in production, not in a demo; we need an engineer who takes a solution all the way to production and measures the result. More about the company — in `brand/Evo-ai_brand_manifesto.md`.

## Must-have requirements

- **ML/LLM experience — 2 years or more.** For juniors/interns — an exception is possible with a strong pet-project portfolio and real drive.
- **Python (confident).**
- **Hands-on experience with LLM APIs** (prompting, function calling) **AND RAG** (embeddings, vector DB).
- **At least one LLM/ML project taken to production in the portfolio.**
- **Readiness for the sprint rhythm** (two-week sprints) **and for the on-call rota.**

## Strong pluses (raise priority)

- **RAG in production** (not just pet projects).
- **One of langchain / llamaindex / an own pipeline.**
- **Quality evaluation** (eval, hallucination/accuracy metrics).
- **English** — reading docs and communicating (conversational at minimum).
- **Open-source / Kaggle contributions / publications.**
- **Integration experience** (helpdesk/CRM/API).
- **Time zone with an overlap ≥ 4 hours with the core team (CET).**

## Additional pluses (nice to have)

- MLOps (Docker, CI/CD, model monitoring).
- Experience in highload / logistics / e-commerce.
- A relevant degree (CS/ML) — but not required.

## What does NOT matter

- A formal diploma (of any kind).
- A specific framework — we will teach it.
- Experience in our exact stack.

## What we offer

- A band of **$2,500–4,000/month** by grade.
- Remote work.
- Paid training/conferences.
- Technology-company status (tax reliefs).

## Selection process

1. **Resume screening** (this stage). The top 5 by fit go to the tech interview.
2. **Technical interview.**
3. **Live coding / take-home assignment.**
4. **Offer.**

## Immediate rejection

- For a senior opening — **not a single project taken to production.**
- **Unwillingness to overlap** with the team.
- **Toxicity.**
