## Our accelerators

Our own in-house work. Every project starts with them and stands on them.

- **Evo-Start — a two-week discovery with success metrics.** We take your task and in two weeks turn it into a clear spec with measurable success metrics, an effort estimate and a go / conditional / no-go decision against the PS-04 checklist. Without this step we do not enter a large project. Guideline price: **from $4,000 / T&M at PS-03 rates.**
- **Evo-RAG — our proven RAG engine.** An in-house search-and-answer engine over a knowledge base: embeddings, vector DB, answers with a link to the source, hallucination control and quality eval. The production foundation of Evo-Assist and Evo-Knowledge — we do not build RAG from scratch every time. **Included in the project; not sold separately.**

## What we do not have

- Chatbots built on rigid buttons and scripted trees.
- Projects without success metrics — if success cannot be measured, we do not take it on.
- "AI for the sake of AI": rollouts for the hype, for a slide to the board, or for a tick in a box.
- Launches without a pilot: no solution goes to production without trial operation.

## What it runs on

- **Inference — vendor ModelAPI** (selected after a comparison: price close to the minimum, 7-day postpay, low minimum order, SLA 99.5%). The backup channel in case of outages or a need for top models — TokenFlow.
- **Search-and-answer engine — our Evo-RAG** (in-house RAG: embeddings, source control, quality eval).
- **Embedding store — a vector DB** (managed, VectoDB).
