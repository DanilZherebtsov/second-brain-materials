# Evo-ai brand manifesto

*Internal document. Version 1.0. Last updated: June 2026.*
*Used as a reference point in hiring, in conversations with clients, in decisions about new projects, and in work with contractors (design, marketing, copywriting).*

---

## What Evo-ai is

A studio that builds AI solutions for business. We build LLM and RAG assistants, document processing, analyst agents — and we take them all the way to production, not to a demo. Founded in 2021. About 25 people, working remotely, core team on CET.

**AI that works in production, not in a demo.**

We connect business and AI — and build a solution that evolves along with the business.

---

## What we do

- **We take on a task that has a success metric.** Not "roll out AI", but "cut handling time", "close part of the requests without escalation", "remove the inconsistency in answers". If there is no metric, we help formulate it before the start.
- **We take it to production.** Anyone can put a demo together. We are accountable for the solution surviving real load, real integrations and real data.
- **We measure the result.** Answer accuracy, latency, self-resolution rate, payback. Numbers, not impressions. On an acceptance set, not by eye.
- **We build a solution that evolves.** The assistant learns from new data, gets retrained, updates the knowledge base. Rollout is the beginning, not the finish line.
- **We are honest about the limits of AI.** Where the model gets it wrong, where it hallucinates, where a human in the loop is needed — we say so directly. A hidden boundary costs more than one named out loud.

## What we do NOT do

- **We do not roll out AI for the hype.** Not "because everyone else has it" and not for a slide to the board. Only for a task with a measurable result.
- **We do not take on work without clear success metrics.** Fixed Price with no clear spec and no acceptance criteria is a stop factor for us.
- **We do not promise a "magic button".** AI is engineering, not magic. There is no button that solves everything on its own.
- **We do not ship a demo that will not survive production.** A beautiful presentation that falls apart under load is not a result.
- **We do not grow project count for the sake of the count.** We take on as much as we can honestly carry to production. Quality of delivery matters more than the number of logos on the website.

## Why Evo-ai

Evo — from "evolution". We do not hand over a frozen system: we build AI that learns and grows along with the business.

ai — that is the connection. Our symbol is two nodes joined by an arc: the business task and the model. We connect them so that they work as one, and we add a rising arc — that same evolution.

A simple move that solves the task: connect business and AI — and make it work in production.

## Tone of voice

Clear, confident, engineering-minded. Respectful and professional. No hype, no buzzwords, no "magic button". Specifics and metrics instead of promises. We explain complex things in plain words, but we do not simplify them into untruths. No emoji in body text.

## Colours and typeface (for contractors)

- **Primary colour:** graphite-indigo, almost black (#171633).
- **Accent 1:** electric violet (#6D5BF7).
- **Accent 2:** teal (#19C7B6).
- **Background:** light and cool, not white (#F5F4FB).
- **Text:** on a light background — #171633; on a dark one — #F5F4FB.
- **Heading typeface:** Space Grotesk (fallback — Sora).
- **Body typeface:** Inter.

No neon, no shouting colours. No Comic Sans, no Times New Roman. The aesthetic is restrained, techno, engineering.
